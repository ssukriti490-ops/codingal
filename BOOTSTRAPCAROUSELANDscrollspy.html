CODING,frequently used interchangeably with computer programming, is the fundamental, iterative,andhighly logical process of designing, writing,testing, and maintaining instructions that allow humans to communicate with machines.At its core, a computer consits of hardware-transistors-that only understand binary code, which is a rapid, on-or-off sequence of zeros and ones. Because writing in binary is impractical for humans, coding serves as a translator,utilizing high-level programming languages ( such as PythonJavaJavaScript****c++,orSwift) that feature syntax more closely resembling humanlanguage.A programmer analyzes a problem,designs an algorithm(a step-by-step logical procedure to solve it),and then translates that algorithm into source code. These instructions, which may range from a few lines for a simple script to millions of lines for complex software, tell the computer precisely how to behave, from rendering a webpage's font color to executing complex mathematical calculations or powering artificial intelligence .
